# Privacy Policy

Cucu Connect is designed to be self-hosted. You control your data.

- We store only what you provide: profile, orders, medications, vitals, etc.
- No analytics or third-party trackers are included.
- You can export all your data via the Profile page (`/api/export/user/:id`).
- You can request deletion by removing your account data from the data store.
- Medication and health data is sensitive; host on a trusted, encrypted server.

If you host Cucu Connect for others, you are responsible for compliance with
applicable health data regulations (e.g. HIPAA, GDPR).
