# Privacy Policy

Cucu Connect is designed to be self-hosted. You control your data.

## Data collected

Only what you provide: profile details, orders, appointments, medications,
vitals, subscriptions, bills, notifications, reviews, and emergency contacts.

## Tracking

No analytics, advertising, or third-party trackers are included.

## Export and deletion

- Export all personal data via the Profile page or
  `GET /api/export/user/:id`.
- Delete account data by removing the user and related records from the data
  store, or by deleting `data/data.json` for a full reset.

## Sensitive health data

Medication and vitals data is sensitive. Host on a trusted server with
encryption at rest and in transit, and restrict access to authorized staff.

## Hosting responsibility

If you host Cucu Connect for others, you are the data controller and are
responsible for compliance with applicable regulations (e.g. HIPAA, GDPR, local
health data laws).
