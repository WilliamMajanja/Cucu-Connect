# Code of Conduct

Cucu Connect is committed to a welcoming, inclusive community. We expect all
participants to treat each other with respect and empathy, especially considering
our users include elderly and disabled people.

## Our standards

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Use inclusive language.
- Accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the community and our users.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, or any conduct that makes others feel unsafe.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to the maintainers. All reports will be reviewed promptly and
confidentially.
